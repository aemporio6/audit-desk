Audit Desk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. Contact: aemporio6@gmail.com

Audit Desk ("the app") is a web page at aemporio6.github.io/audit-desk that helps online sellers check their marketplace reports, supplier costs and bank statements. It has no server of its own.

What the app does with your Google account

When you press "Sign in with Google", the app asks for permission to use your own Google Drive and to know your e-mail address. It uses this only to: (1) save the app's own data file ("Audit Desk data.json") and daily backups in your Drive; (2) read the reports, spreadsheets and folders that you choose to give it (for example a folder or link you paste); (3) show your e-mail address so you know which account is connected.

Where your data goes

Everything is processed inside your web browser. Your files and results are stored in your browser on your device and in your own Google Drive. We do not receive, copy, store or see your data; there is no Audit Desk server or database. Your data is not sold, not shared with advertisers and not shared with any third party. If you share a folder or add a team member, that person receives only what you choose to share, through Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Audit Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people except where you share it.

Optional encryption

You can lock your Drive data with a passphrase (AES-GCM, in your browser). If you lose the passphrase, we cannot recover it because we never have it.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete the app's files from your Drive at any time, clear your browser's site data, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops the app from reading your Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear above.
